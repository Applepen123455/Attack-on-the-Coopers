ATTACK ON THE COOPERS

The Cooper family needs your help! Control your favourite young Sheldon character and help them escape from the dungeon.

How to Win

1. Explore rooms and survive enemy waves.
2. Collect 3 relic keys from marked rooms.
3. Defeat the Warden Core boss.
4. Bring the core back to the starting room to escape.

Enemy clues: red laser = sniper, orange pulse = bomber, green beam = healer, blue ring = shield unit.
